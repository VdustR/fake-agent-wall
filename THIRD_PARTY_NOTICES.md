## iTerm2 Color Schemes

The bundled terminal colour presets were converted from the Windows Terminal
schemes in [`mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes`](https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes).
The collection is distributed under the MIT License. Individual colour schemes
remain subject to the rights of their original authors.

The normalized data format follows
[`VdustR/term-ptt-custom-theme`](https://github.com/VdustR/term-ptt-custom-theme),
which is distributed under the MIT License.
